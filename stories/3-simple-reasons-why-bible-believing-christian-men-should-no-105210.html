3 Simple Reasons Why Bible-Believing Christian Men Should Not be Marching Under the Leader of the Nation of Islam, Louis Farrakhan

October 10, 2015 Christian

The editor of BCNN1 believed the following and made similar statements 20 years ago when the original march was taking place.

1. You are a Christian. Muslims don’t believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God and the Savior of the world. You do. You are supposed to believe what the Bible says. You are not a Muslim and you do not believe what Muslims believe. Ephesians 5:11 says Christians should “have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather reprove them.”

2. Louis Farrakhan has made some good statements regarding the black community that all black and Christian people can agree with. For example, we agree with Farrakhan when he encourages young black men to stop killing each other and to stop committing crimes so they can avoid going to jail. However, there are some statements that he has made that Christians cannot agree with. For example, his recent statements at a church in Florida in which he called for ‘10,000 fearless men’ to ‘rise up and kill those who kill us.’

3. Down through the years, Louis Farrakhan and the Nation of Islam has joined other Muslims against Israel, the apple of God’s eye. We, as Christians, ought to stand with Israel. God promised Israel, “I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.”

Why is it that the ‘Nation of Christianity’ does not have a leader who can bring together a group of one, two, or three million men to stand against the evil that is happening in this nation? Perhaps, Christian leaders such as Tony Evans, T.D. Jakes, Paul Morton, and others can get together to lead one million black men in marching, not under the banner of the Nation of Islam, but under the blood-stained banner of the Nation of Christianity.

First Peter 2:9 says, “But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light.”

–BCNN1 Editors
