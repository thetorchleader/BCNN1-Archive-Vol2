3-Year-Old Boy Was on Park Swing for 2 Days Before He Was Found Dead; Death Ruled a Homicide

June 30, 2015 National

Mother Romechia Simms, 24, and her son, Ji’Aire Lee, 3, are seen after Lee was taken in March for his first haircut. (Courtesy of family)

The story was chilling and heartbreaking: A 3-year-old boy was found dead on a swing set, his mother pushing him on the swing.

More than a month after Ji’Aire Donnell Lee’s death, police revealed more of the terrible story: Lee was alive when his mother sat him down on the swing. He stayed on it for almost two whole days.

A medical examiner’s office has ruled that Lee’s death was a homicide. He died due to hypothermia and dehydration, the Charles County Sheriff’s Office said Tuesday.

The sheriff’s office said that the autopsy report will be sent to the Charles County state’s attorney’s office, which will decide whether to file charges against Lee’s mother, Romechia Simms.

Lee was dead when police found his mother pushing him on a swing in Wills Memorial Park. At the time, police were unsure when and how Lee died.
