16-Year-Old and 18-Year-Old Chicago Teens Charged with Fatally Shooting Mother of Three

March 13, 2016 Black

Natrell Jackson (Chicago Police)

An 11th grader and an 18-year-old man were charged with fatally shooting a mother of three small children as she sat in a cab just south of U.S. Cellular Field last month. 

Michael Whitehead received no bail Saturday during a hearing at the Leighton Criminal Court Building. The 16-year-old was charged with first-degree murder in the Feb. 26 shooting death of Shari Graham near the Wentworth Garden Homes on the South Side.

Also charged with muder is Natrell Jackson, 18, of the 100 block of E. 59th Street, according to Chicago police. Jackson who was not in court Saturday, is slated to appear Monday, according to Chicago Police Officer Michelle Tannehill, a spokeswoman for the police.

Graham, 30, was the 101st person killed this year during a particularly violent winter.

Dressed in a navy blue Juvenile Temporary Detention Center sweatsuit, Whitehead stood before the judge, his right handing grasping his left arm behind his back.

Prosecutors described the shooting in the 3800 block of South Princeton Avenue that killed Graham — mother of 14-month-old and 3-year-old sons and a 6-year-old daughter struck in the back as she sat in a cab — as stray gunfire meant for another vehicle believed to be gang rivals. Police identified Whitehead as a member of the Gangster Disciples gang, according to an arrest report.

Graham hired a “civilian taxi” to take her to a nearby fast food restaurant to get food for herself and her boyfriend when the shooting occurred, prosecutors said.

Surveillance video from the Chicago Housing Authority captured Whitehead and another gunman open fire in the direction of the a car believed to be carrying three people, Wang said in court.

After firing a combined 12 shots, both gunmen and a witness ran back into the Wentworth Garden Homes while nearby Graham sat wounded in the back of a “civilian taxi,” according to Wang.
