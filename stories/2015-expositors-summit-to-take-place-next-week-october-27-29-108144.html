2015 Expositors Summit to Take Place Next Week, October 27-29, at Southern Baptist Theological Seminary

October 25, 2015 Christian

The proper preaching of the Word of God is an indispensable foundation of a healthy church. For those preaching, it is imperative that the point of the biblical text is the point of the sermon. It is for this reason the Expositors’ Summit is designed to instruct and equip preachers and students for the glorious task of expository ministry.

Also, don’t miss out on the 2015 Pre-Conference: The Pulpit and Reconciliation.

SPEAKERS

R. Albert Mohler Jr.

R. Albert Mohler Jr. is the president of The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary. He is an esteemed authority on contemporary issues and has been recognized by such influential publications as Time and Christianity Today as a leader among American evangelicals.

In addition to his presidential duties, Dr. Mohler hosts two programs: “The Briefing,” a daily analysis of news and events from a Christian worldview; and “Thinking in Public,” a series of conversations with the day’s leading thinkers. He also writes a popular blog and a regular commentary on moral, cultural and theological issues. Full Bio.

HB Charles Jr.

Pastor H.B. Charles Jr. is the pastor of Shiloh Metropolitan Baptist Church in Jacksonville, Florida. Pastor Charles is a contributing author to Power in the Pulpit and he speaks regularly at revivals, conferences, and other church events around the country. Full Bio.

Derek Thomas

Derek Thomas is the Senior Minister of Preaching and Teaching at The First Presbyterian Church of Columbia in South Carolina. Dr. Thomas is also the Robert Strong Professor of Systematic and Pastoral Theology at Reformed Theological Seminary Atlanta and the Editorial Director of The Alliance of Confessing Evangelicals. He and his wife Rosemary have two children and two grandchildren. Full Bio.
