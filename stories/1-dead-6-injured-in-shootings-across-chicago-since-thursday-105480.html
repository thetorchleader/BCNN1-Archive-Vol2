1 Dead, 6 Injured in Shootings Across Chicago Since Thursday

October 11, 2015 Black

© Scott Olson/Getty Images File photo of Chicago police cruisers.

One man was killed and at least six other people were wounded in shootings Thursday, police said.

At about 8:30 p.m., a 36-year-old man was fatally shot and a 50-year-old man was wounded in the South Austin neighborhood, said Officer Thomas Sweeney, a Chicago police spokesman.

The two were shot in the 5800 block of West Chicago Avenue in what may have been a robbery during a dice game, police said. Someone drove them to West Suburban Medical Center a few blocks away.

Other shootings:

-A 32-year-old man was wounded in the 7100 block of South South Shore Drive when shots were fired from a passing white sedan around 11:35 p.m., according to Officer Amina Greer, a Chicago police spokesman.

He was struck in the arm and taken to Jackson Park Hospital. His condition was stabilized.

-At 11:30 p.m., a 26-year-old man was shot in West Garfield Park, police said.

He was outside in the 4000 block of West Monroe Street when he heard gunfire and felt pain, Greer said. He was hit multiple times in the leg and taken to Mount Sinai Hospital, where his condition was stabilized.

-At 6:45 p.m., an 18-year-old man was shot in the Roseland neighborhood, Sweeney said.

He was in the 300 block of West 109th Street when people walked up and began shooting, police said. He was hit in the leg and taken in good condition to Roseland Community Hospital, Sweeney said.

-Earlier Thursday, two men were wounded within minutes of each other in the Austin neighborhood.

The first shooting happened about 1:20 p.m. in the 4800 block of West Iowa Street, said Officer Janel Sedevic, a Chicago police spokeswoman.

A 23-year-old man said he was walking when he heard shots and felt pain. He suffered a graze wound to the head and was taken in good condition to Mount Sinai Hospital, Sedevic said.

About 10 minutes later, a 25-year-old man was wounded in the 5100 block of West Division Street, Sedevic said.

He suffered a wound to the arm and was taken to Mount Sinai, where he was listed in good condition.

Source: Chicago Tribune | Deanese Williams-Harris and Megan Crepeau
