1 Dead, 8 Wounded in Explosion and Fire at Building in Brooklyn, NY

October 3, 2015 National

An explosion in a three-story building in Borough Park, Brooklyn, killed one person and injured eight others on Saturday. Credit Robert Stolarik for The New York Times

A woman was killed and eight people were injured on Saturday when an explosion and fire ripped through a three-story building in the Borough Park section of Brooklyn, emergency officials said.

The Fire Department said one resident of the building remained unaccounted for as marshals worked to determine what caused the blast. Officials said a gas leak was strongly suspected.

Mayor Bill de Blasio said at a news conference on Saturday that three civilians were hospitalized following the blast, including one person in critical condition. Their injuries were not considered life-threatening, he said. Five firefighters were treated for minor injuries at the scene.

“It’s obviously a very tough day here in Borough Park,” he said, adding: “This is a tragedy.”

Emergency personnel received a report of an explosion and fire at 4206 13th Avenue around 1:05 p.m., the Fire Department said. The mayor said that more than 200 firefighters responded to the fire, which badly damaged at least two other buildings and was still smoldering on Saturday evening.

Commissioner Daniel A. Nigro said at the news conference that investigators believed the blast occurred on the second floor, where a tenant was replacing a stove connected to the building’s gas line.

The impact of the explosion blew off most of the building’s facade and rocked the block, officials and residents said. The charred, shattered and soaked remains of the tenants’ belongings were visible from the street. Bricks tumbled from neighboring buildings as firefighters tried to mitigate the damage.

Comparing the explosion to blasts since last year in Harlem and the East Village, which were both linked to gas leaks, Gov. Andrew M. Cuomo ordered the state’s Department of Public Service to investigate the cause of the explosion.
