20 Best Places to Live in America

March 12, 2016 From Around the Block

Americans will move across the country for just about anything – be it for a job, to be closer to a loved one or just to get a little more sun. While the initial reason for a move to a new city will vary from person to person, the location’s job market, affordability and overall quality of life are a few of the characteristics to consider before the movers are called.

The 2016 U.S. News Best Places to Live rankings examine a variety of factors, from job market to quality of life, that ultimately determine which of the country’s largest metro areas would be more suitable for more people.
