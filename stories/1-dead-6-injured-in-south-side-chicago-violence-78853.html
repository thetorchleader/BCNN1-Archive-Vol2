1 Dead, 6 Injured in South Side Chicago Violence

June 9, 2015 Black

Police investigate the scene of shooting on the 700 block of South Francisco Avenue. (Michael Noble Jr., Chicago Tribune)

One man was killed and at least six other people were wounded in shootings since Monday afternoon and evening on the South and West sides, according to police.

A man was found fatally shot in the Brainerd neighborhood about 7 p.m., said Chicago Police Department spokesman Officer Thomas Sweeney.

The man, age 35, was discovered unresponsive in the 9200 block of South Aberdeen Street, and was found to have multiple gunshot wounds to the chest and head, Sweeney said. He was taken to Advocate Christ Medical Center in Oak Lawn, where he was pronounced dead.

Most recently, a 37-year-old man was shot in the 8400 block of South Parnell Avenue in the Gresham neighborhood early Tuesday morning.

The man was sitting inside a parked white Mercedes sedan in a parking lot of a housing complex when a gunman pulled up in a black vehicle, got out and began shooting, according to police and witnesses. The 37-year-old tried to drive away but crashed into several parked cars and into the side of an apartment building before finally running away from the gunman.

Police said the man fled to a relative’s house and was driven to Advocate Christ Medical Center in Oak Lawn. He was treated for a gunshot wound to his left arm and was listed in good condition as of Tuesday morning.
