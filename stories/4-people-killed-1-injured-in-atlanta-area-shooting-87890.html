4 People Killed, 1 Injured in Atlanta-area Shooting

July 22, 2015 National

Suburban Atlanta sheriff’s officials say four people have been killed, with one injured at a home here Wednesday.

The Forsyth County Sheriff’s Office posted on Facebook that they believe the shooting incident in Suwanee, about 33 miles north of Atlanta, was possibly a domestic-related murder suicide.

Forsyth County Sheriff’s Maj. Rick Doyle, director of operations for the department, said authorities think the shootings occurred around 6 a.m. Wednesday in the residence along Old Atlanta Road.

The identities of those involved were not immediately released.

Doyle said the suspect in the case is known among local law officers from previous arrests. Police said there is no threat to the public with no suspect on the loose.

The area where authorities think the shooting occurred, about 30 miles outside the city, includes some of metro Atlanta’s most exclusive neighborhoods. Old Atlanta Road winds past golf course communities and expansive homes in a heavily wooded area.

Contributing: The Associated Press
