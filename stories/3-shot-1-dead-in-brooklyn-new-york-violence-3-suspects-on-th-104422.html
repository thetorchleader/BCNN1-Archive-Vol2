3 Shot, 1 Dead in Brooklyn, New York, Violence; 3 Suspects on the Loose

October 6, 2015 Black

© Provided by New York Daily News Three people were shot, one fatally, as gunfire rang out on a Brooklyn street early Tuesday morning. – Joe Marino/New York Daily News

Three people were shot — one fatally — as gunfire erupted on a Brooklyn street Tuesday morning, officials said.

Shots rang out on Grattan St. near Knickerbocker Ave. in Bushwick about 7:50 a.m., hitting two men and a woman, police said.

One of the men died at the scene, police said.

The other man suffered a graze wound to the chest and the woman was grazed in the hip, cops said. The two surviving victims were taken to Elmhurst Hospital.

Cops were looking for three suspects who fled the scene, police sources said.
