70-Year-Old Pastor Shot and Killed by His Brother at Ohio Church

February 28, 2016 Black, National

A shooting occurred inside St. Peter’s Missionary Baptist Church in Ohio on Sunday. (PHOTO CREDIT: Google Street View)

A pastor was shot during a Sunday service at an Ohio church and his brother may be the gunman, WDTN reported.

The Montgomery County Coroner’s Office identified the Pastor as William Schooler, 70, of St. Peter’s Missionary Baptist Church.

Dayton Police are believed to have the suspected shooter in custody, according to witnesses who saw cops remove a man in handcuffs and place him in the back of a cruiser.

The pastor had just walked toward the pulpit when his brother, who was seated in a pew, stood up and followed him, witnesses told WDTN. Soon after, several shots were fired and the congregation went running.
