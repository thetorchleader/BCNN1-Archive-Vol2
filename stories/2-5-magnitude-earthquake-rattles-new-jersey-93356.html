2.5 Magnitude Earthquake Rattles New Jersey

August 14, 2015 National

This is what this morning’s 2.5 magnitude earthquake in NJ looked like. Residents say it sounded like an explosion. (Nora Muchanic/Twitter)

A 2.5 magnitude earthquake was reported in Somerset County, New Jersey early Friday morning.

The U.S. Geological Survey says the magnitude-2.5 quake hit around 3:41 a.m. Friday, roughly 2 miles north of Bernardsville at a depth of 3 miles.

Police say there are no reports of damage.

USGS geophysicist Zachary Reeves says 78 people reported feeling it.

Reeves says such small earthquakes usually don’t cause damage, but people will usually feel a little bit of shaking on higher floors.

The USGS says smaller earthquakes are felt in the region every two to three years.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
