5 Students Stabbed at University of California, Merced; Suspect Shot and Killed

November 4, 2015 Education, National

© Ben Margot/AP File photo of the University of California at Merced campus in Merced, Calif.

Five students were stabbed Wednesday at the University of California, Merced, and the suspect was fatally shot by police, the school said.

“Update: 5 students stabbed; 2 sent out via medical helicopter; 3 treated on campus. All conscious.” the school tweeted.

The school later tweeted that the suspect had been killed by officers at the scene. The campus, located in the San Joaquin Valley, was closed and classes were canceled for the day.

The stabbing attack took place in front of a classroom, officials said.

“The situation is under control, the school said on its website. The school issued a statement saying counseling would be available on campus.

The attack quickly drew students to social media.

“5 people got stabbed on campus today. Classes are cancelled and I’m locked inside my building. I just wanna be home right now” tweeted Taylor Kim. Minutes later Kim added: “I hope everyone here is okay.”

Tweeted Alyson Garcia: “Stuck on campus, but I really just want to go home…”

UC Merced has an enrollment of more than 6,000 students. The school, on 7,000 acres about 60 miles northwest of Fresno, opened for graduate students in 2004 and undergrads a year later.

Source: USA Today | John Bacon
