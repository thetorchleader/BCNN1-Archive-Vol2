10-Year-Old Injured in 20 Shootings Across Chicago Since Sunday

September 29, 2015 Black

© Alexandra Chachkevitch/Chicago Tribune/TNS Police officers investigate the scene of the shooting in the 5200 block of South Justine Street where four people were wounded late Sunday evening on Sept. 27, 2015 in Chicago.

A man was killed and at least 19 other people, including a 10-year-old girl, have been wounded since Sunday afternoon in Chicago shootings, police said.

A 24-year-old man died after being shot at 2:02 p.m. on the 3900 block of Roosevelt Road in the Lawndale neighborhood, said Officer Veejay Zala, a Chicago police spokesman.

The man was shot in the back and shoulder and was later pronounced dead, police said.

Most recently, a 35-year-old man and a 34-year-old man were wounded in a shooting around 2:49 a.m. Monday in the Belmont Central neighborhood on the Northwest Side, said Chicago Police Department spokesman Officer Ron Gaines.

The two men were near the intersection of Grand and Mango avenues when they were shot. The 35-year-old was shot twice in the back and was listed in serious condition, Gaines said. The 34-year-old was shot in the stomach, and his exact condition was immediately unknown, Gaines said.

Police officers responding to a call of people shot in the area saw an attacker and started running after him in the 2200 block of North Major Avenue, Gaines said. During the foot chase, police fired shots, but no one was wounded, Gaines said.

Police took a male into custody.

Early Monday morning, police officers blocked off the entrance and the sidewalk in front of Jerez Sports Bar & Grill with yellow crime tape. Officers went in and out of the bar as they investigated the crime scene.

Around midnight, a 23-year-old man walked into Rush University Medical Center after getting shot in the leg in the West Elsdon neighborhood on the Southwest Side, Gaines said.

The man was in the 4000 block of West 55th Street when he was shot. He was transferred from Rush to Stroger Hospital, where his condition stabilized, police said.

About 11:20 p.m. Sunday, a 21-year-old man was wounded in a shooting in the North Austin neighborhood on the West Side, said Chicago Police Department spokeswoman Officer Janel Sedevic.

The man was standing on the corner in the 5000 block of West Division Street when someone fired shots at him from a passing vehicle, Sedevic said.

The man was taken to Mount Sinai Hospital, where his condition stabilized, Sedevic said.

About 10:20 p.m., a 50-year-old man was shot in the leg while trying to break a fight in the Humboldt Park neighborhood on the Northwest Side, Gaines said.

The man was at Humboldt Park in the 1300 block of Luis Munoz Marin Drive when he attempted to break a fight between two people, Gaines said. One of the people then shot him in the leg.

The 50-year-old was taken to Mount Sinai Hospital, where his condition stabilized, Gaines said.

Police took a suspect into custody, but no official charges have been released as of early Monday morning.

Around 10:40 p.m., three men were seriously hurt and a woman was wounded in a drive-by shooting in the Back of the Yards neighborhood on the South Side, said Chicago Police Department spokeswoman Officer Amina Greer.

The four people were standing on a sidewalk in the 5200 block of South Justine Street when someone pulled up to them in a vehicle and fired shots, Greer said.

A 35-year-old man was hit in the leg and hip. A 33-year-old man was shot in the hip area, and a 30-year-old man was struck in the groin. All three men were listed in serious condition at Stroger Hospital, Greer said.
