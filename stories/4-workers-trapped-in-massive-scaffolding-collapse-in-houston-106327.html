4 Workers Trapped in Massive Scaffolding Collapse in Houston

October 16, 2015 National

Firemen on scene at a scaffold collapse in Houston, TX on Oct. 15, 2015. (Source: KPRC)

At least four workers were trapped in a massive scaffolding collapse at a construction site in downtown Houston on Friday, local media reported.

Live video from the scene provided by KHOU and others showed the collapsed scaffolding along a city block. One worker was shown being pulled from the wreckage and placed on a stretcher.

(Reporting by Jon Herskovitz in Austin and the Houston Bureau; Editing by Mohammad Zargham)

SOURCE: Reuters

______

Emergency workers in downtown Houston were trying to rescue at least four construction workers who were trapped after a scaffolding collapsed Friday morning, according to a report.

The incident occurred at an apartment complex under construction directly across from Minute Maid Park — home of baseball’s Houston Astros, according to NBC TV affiliate KPRC, a local news station in Houston.

The Houston Fire Department said it had more than 30 units on scene, including several heavy rescue units.

Three workers have been rescued and taken to the hospital with non-life-threatening injuries and another two were treated on the scene, Houston Fire Department Captain Ruy Lozano said. Officials were unsure if there were more people trapped beneath the wreckage.
