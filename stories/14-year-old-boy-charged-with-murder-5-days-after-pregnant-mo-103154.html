14-Year-Old Boy Charged With Murder 5 Days After Pregnant Mother is Found Dead on Kitchen Floor

September 29, 2015 National

Tragic: Nita Moseley, 33, (pictured) was found dead on her kitchen floor on Tuesday. Five days later, her teenage son was charged with her murder

A 14-year-old boy has been charged with murdering his pregnant mother five days after she was found dead inside their family home.

Nita Moseley, 33, was lying on the kitchen floor in La Marque, Texas, on Tuesday when a neighbor dropped by the visit.

Her son, who has not been named, was not in the house, prompting a five-day manhunt.

According to the Houston Chronicle, the academic football player was taken to the local police station on Sunday evening.

He was charged on Monday morning after interviews with detectives.

In a news statement, police said the boy was cooperating with the investigation.

They believe he acted alone.

According to neighbors, Moseley was four months pregnant when she was found dead.

The neighbor who found her told KHOU: ‘Saturday morning, when I drive by and I saw the door open, that’s when I figured something was wrong.

‘I thought maybe someone had broken in the house or something and that’s when I found the unfortunate event that happened.
