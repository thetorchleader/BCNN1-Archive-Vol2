18 Injured After Two Buses Collide In Lincoln Tunnel

June 10, 2015 National

Emergency workers placed injured commuters on stretchers after two buses collided in the Lincoln Tunnel. Credit Michael Appleton for The New York Times

More than a dozen people were injured when two buses collided in the Lincoln Tunnel on Wednesday morning, officials said. The crash set off major traffic problems with reports of drivers and passengers stuck in the tunnel for well over an hour.

The crash occurred around 9:30 a.m. in the tunnel’s center tube when a New Jersey Transit bus hit the back of a private bus, said Joseph Pentangelo, a spokesman for the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey. At least 18 people reported injuries that did not appear to be life-threatening and were taken to local hospitals, he said.

Both buses were traveling eastbound and were near the Manhattan side of the tunnel when the crash occurred. The collision is under investigation, Mr. Pentangelo said.

The Route 166 bus had about 60 passengers on board, said Nancy Snyder, a spokeswoman for New Jersey Transit.

Drivers were warned to avoid the tunnel and to expect significant delays in the area. Manhattan-bound traffic was being rerouted to the south tube of the tunnel.

Bus service to and from the Port Authority Bus Terminal was expected to have delays of up to 45 minutes, New Jersey Transit said.
