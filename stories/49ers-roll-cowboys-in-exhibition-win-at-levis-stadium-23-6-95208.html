49ers Roll Cowboys in Exhibition Win at Levi’s Stadium, 23-6

August 24, 2015 Sports

“San Francisco 49ers’ Kendall Gaskins (40) is tackled by the Dallas Cowboys in the fourth quarter of their NFL preseason game at Levi’s Stadium in Santa Clara, Calif., on Sunday, Aug. 23, 2015. (Nhat V. Meyer/Bay Area News Group)”

NaVorro Bowman lined up on Levi’s Stadium’s left hash mark at 5:05 p.m., darted through the Dallas Cowboys line of scrimmage and made a storybook-like tackle to reboot his career.

It was the moment so many waited for from the 49ers star linebacker, who suffered a catastrophic knee injury in the NFC Championship game loss at Seattle in January 2014.

Bowman didn’t just come back Sunday in a 23-6 exhibition win, as he came back again and again to influence the next two snaps and force a Cowboys punt.

Bowman’s three-snap cameo provided the early thrills, and Australian import Jarryd Hayne stole the spotlight from there as a punt returner and backup running back. Adding to the rout were touchdowns from unlikely sources: an interception return by 303-pound nose tackle Mike Purcell and a blocked punt in the end zone by Quinton Patton.

Bowman set the tone for a celebratory reopening of the 49ers’ embattled field, where the only development about the new sod was how well the 49ers played on it, including Bowman.

“He responded great,” coach Jim Tomsula said. “I thought I was going to have to call time out to get him off the field. I’m glad it was a three-and-out.”

Tomsula said Bowman wouldn’t have played more than five snaps, as the coach wanted to let him get readapted to the game-day process physically and psychologically.

It had been 581 days since Bowman last suited up for a game. Unable to return last season, Bowman also did not play Aug. 15 in the exhibition opener at Houston.

“It’s a huge relief,” Bowman said. “I can squat down and take some contact on the knee and move as quick as the guy running the ball.”

As for playing only three snaps, Bowman said: “I wanted to be out there longer. But it’s early in the year, and we have a long road ahead of us. We want to be smart with it.”

On his second snap, Bowman burst through on a blitz, helping Aaron Lynch and Nick Moody produce a tackle for a 1-yard loss on Darren McFadden, who gained 1 yard on the Cowboys’ first play.
