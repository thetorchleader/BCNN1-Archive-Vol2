3 Dead, 10 Wounded Since Friday Night in Chicago

March 13, 2016 Black

Police tape at an armored car robbery. | Brian Jackson~Sun-Times

Three men were killed and at least 17 other people have been wounded in shootings across Chicago since Friday evening, according to Chicago Police. 

The latest homicide happened early Saturday in the Avondale neighborhood on the Northwest Side.

Ricardo Montes, 22, was on a sidewalk in the 3200 block of West Addison about 3:30 a.m. when someone opened fire from a passing SUV, police and the Cook County medical examiner’s office said.

Montes, who lived in the 3700 block of North Albany, was shot in the back and taken to Advocate Illinois Masonic Medical Center, where he later died, authorities said.

Friday night, a 24-year-old man was gunned down in the West Garfield Park neighborhood.

William Smith was standing outside in the 3800 block of West Flournoy shortly after 8 p.m. when someone walked up and shot him in the head, according to police and the medical examiner’s office.

Smith was taken to Mount Sinai Hospital, where he died less than two hours later, authorities said. He lived in the 900 block of North Springfield.

The weekend’s first fatal shooting happened about 5:45 p.m. Friday in the West Side Austin neighborhood.

A 20-year-0ld man was shot several times while sitting in a vehicle in the 800 block of North Latrobe Avenue, police said.

He was taken to Stroger Hospital, where he later died. The medical examiner’s office withheld his name Saturday night pending notification of his family.

The most recent nonfatal shooting happened Saturday night in the North Lawndale neighborhood on the Southwest Side.

Two 16-year-old boys and a 19-year-old man were standing in the street in the 4000 block of West 18th Street about 9:35 p.m. when they heard shots and realized they had been struck, police said. One of the 16-year-old boys was shot in the right leg, the other suffered a gunshot wound to the right leg and left arm and the 19-year-old man was shot in both legs. They were taken to Mount Sinai Hospital, but their conditions were not known, police said.

About 6:35 p.m. Saturday, a man was shot near the CTA’s Morse Red Line station in the Rogers Park neighborhood on the North Side. The 18-year-old man was standing outside in the 1400 block of West Morse Avenue at 6:35 p.m. when he heard shots and realized he had been struck in the leg, police said. He was taken to Presence Saint Francis Hospital in Evanston, where his condition was stabilized.
