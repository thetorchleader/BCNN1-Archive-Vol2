7 People Shot in Washington, D.C., During Labor Day Weekend

September 8, 2015 Black

WASHINGTON, DC- District of Columbia Police investigate the scene of a shooting this summer. The city’s homicide tally ticked up from 105 to 108 over the Labor Day weekend. (Amanda Voisard/For the Washington Post)

Seven people were shot and wounded within an hour late Monday as gunfire punctuated an already deadly holiday weekend in the nation’s capital.

At about 10 p.m., dozens of shots were fired in the 2900 block of Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue SE, a rundown corridor of convenience stores just south of the new headquarters for the U.S. Coast Guard and the city’s new homeland security headquarters.

When the gunfire stopped, five adult men lay bleeding from wounds that ranged from minor to serious in nature, police said.

About 30 minutes later, gunfire erupted about two miles to the south, along Forrester Street SW. Two adult men in that exchange also suffered serious injuries, police said.

Even before Monday night, the District’s homicide tally had ticked up from 105 to 108 over the long holiday weekend, surging well beyond last year’s tally of 104 for all of 2014.

The violent weekend offered no reprieve for D.C. Mayor Muriel E. Bowser and Police Chief Cathy L. Lanier, who have faced criticism of shifting explanations for a jump in deadly shootings this year. Through early September, homicides are up 40 compared to the same time period last year.

Bowser announced Monday that she and Lanier would address the violence in a news conference Tuesday morning.
