1 Person Dead After Chicago Bus Crashes Into 4 Cars

June 3, 2015 National

An accident June 2, 2015, involving a Chicago city bus and multiple cars sent several people to the hospital. (perroradio/Instagram)

One person is dead and eight others were injured Tuesday in a crash involving a city bus and multiple cars, Chicago officials said.

The Chicago Transit Authority bus was turning off Lake Street where it intersects with Michigan Avenue, collided with four cars and came to a stop on a pedestrian plaza, ABC station WLS in Chicago reported.

Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office spokesman Frank Shuftan said Northwestern Memorial Hospital reported that one of the people brought there after the crash died.

The bus driver was later ticketed for failing to stop at a red light and failing to exercise due care, Chicago Police told ABC News.
