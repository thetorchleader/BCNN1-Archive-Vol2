2015: A Year In Which We Finally Talked About Race

December 22, 2015 Black

Barack Obama is seen as one of the most polarizing presidents in American history, according to numerous polls. Much of that polarization revolves around race.

The issue of race took center stage in 2015 in old and new ways — from questions about policing in Ferguson, Missouri, and Baltimore, Chicago and elsewhere to a gunman’s massacre at a black church in Charleston, South Carolina. Our first black president was judged with every word he said — and those he didn’t. And the 50th anniversary of the civil rights march in Selma, Alabama, reminded us that when it comes to race, we’ve come a long way — and have far to go.
