2015 Annual Redeemed Women’s Conference Begins August 12th

August 9, 2015 Black

Dr. Jacqueline Martin

Dr. Jacqueline Martin, Evangelist Audrey Williams and the Redeemed Women’s Ministry of Love, Peace and Happiness (LPH) Family Christian Fellowship will kick-off their 2015 Annual Redeemed Women’s Conference on Wednesday, August 12, at 6 p.m., through Sunday, August 16, at 4 p.m. 

The event takes place at the LPH Church located at 11022 Old River School Road in Downey. The theme is ‘Women Empowering Women for Kingdom Greatness.’

The weekday agenda includes prayer at 6 p.m., followed by worship services at 7:15 p.m. The conference speakers are Evangelist Darlene Patterson, Pastor Janet Rodgers and Pastor Carlinda Lowery. On Sunday, Evangelist Audrey Williams will preach.
