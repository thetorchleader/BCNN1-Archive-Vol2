32 Ways to Do Red Velvet for Valentine’s Day

February 6, 2016 Lifestyle

Its national popularity may have reached an apex only in recent years, but legend holds that red velvet cake got its start in the Waldorf Astoria’s kitchens way back in the interwar years. Today, the hotel offers this decadent, blood-red dessert in both cake and cupcake forms. Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts

The ultimate vehicle for cream cheese in all its manifestations, red velvet is the romantic result of food coloring or beetroot added to chocolate or dark chocolate desserts, but the presentation has become a flavor of its own. Traditionally a take on cake (said to have originated at Waldorf Astoria New York), pastry chefs and artisans alike add their interpretations to all kinds of menu items around Valentine’s Day. This year, you can celebrate sipping red velvet tea, beer or wine, or indulge in doughnuts, churros, whoopie pies and more.
