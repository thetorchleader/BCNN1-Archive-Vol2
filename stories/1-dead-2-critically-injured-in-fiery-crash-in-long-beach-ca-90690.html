WATCH: 1 Dead, 2 Critically Injured in Fiery Crash in Long Beach, CA

August 2, 2015 National

Police are seen at the Gerald Desmond Bridge in Long Beach after a fatal crash closed it in both directions on Aug. 1, 2015. (Credit: KTLA)

One person died, two were in critical condition and a police officer was injured after a wrong-way driver was involved in a three-vehicle crash that prompted an hourslong closure of the Gerald Desmond Bridge in Long Beach on Saturday morning, officials said.

Earlier in the day, officers saw a Mercedes-Benz “driving erratically and crashing through barricades in the area of Special Olympics events,” according to a news release from the Long Beach Police Department. A half-marathon was scheduled for 8 a.m. from Alamitos Beach to East Shoreline Drive and Linden Avenue, about 3 miles from the bridge.

Police lost sight of the vehicle after attempting to follow it along a bike path in the city’s downtown area.

About 7:11 a.m., officers responded to reports of a crash in the eastbound lanes of the bridge at its crest. They attempted to rescue the drivers of two vehicles that were on fire, the statement said.

The driver of a 2010 Nissan pickup truck succumbed to his injuries and was pronounced dead at the scene, according to police. The man’s name was not immediately released, pending notification of family by the Los Angeles County coroner’s office.

An officer suffered minor injuries to his arm when he had tried to extricate the victim, and was subsequently taken to a hospital, said Jake Heflin of the Long Beach Fire Department. He was treated and released.

Firefighters at the scene extinguished the flames and transported the surviving two drivers to hospitals in critical condition, the news release said.
