6 Dead, 3 Injured In String of Random Shootings In Michigan

February 21, 2016 National

At least six people have died and three others injured when a gunman opened fire in random locations in Michigan Saturday, police said.

Kalamazoo County Undersheriff Paul Matyas told Fox 17 Online that people were killed at a Cracker Barrel restaurant in Texas Township, while two others were killed at Seelye Kia.

An 8-year-old child was among those killed at the restaurant, according to WOOD-TV. Officials said a father and a son were killed at the dealership.

The gunman is described as a white male in his 50s and was seen driving a blue Chevy HHR, according to Fox 17 Online. He’s still at large as of early Sunday and appears to be driving around targeting people.

Police believe the shootings are related to an earlier drive-by shooting at Meadows Townhomes, which left one woman injured.

SOURCE: FOX News
The Associated Press contributed to this report.
