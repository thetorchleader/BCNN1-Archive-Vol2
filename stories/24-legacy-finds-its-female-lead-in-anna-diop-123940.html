’24: Legacy’ Finds Its Female Lead In Anna Diop

February 19, 2016 Entertainment

20 May 2014, Los Angeles, California, USA — CBS TV Studios Summer Soiree held at The London Hotel in Los Angeles, CA. May 19, 2014. Pictured: Anna Diop — Image by © Fitzroy Barrett/Splash News/Corbis

Anna Diop will star in Fox’s upcoming 24 reboot, Deadline reports. The Messengers alum will play Nicole, the wife of Corey Hawkins’ Eric Carter.

24: Legacy will focus on Carter, a former Army Ranger who runs into trouble when he returns to the U.S. Carter turns to CTU for help in saving his own life and stopping a potential large-scale terror attack on American soil. Homeland‘s Miranda Otto will play the former head of CTU Rebecca Ingram, who is having second thoughts about resigning her post.

In addition to Diop’s role on The Messengers, she has also appeared in Quantico, Touch and Everybody Hates Chris.

SOURCE: TVGuide – Sadie Gennis
