27-Year-Old Bronx Mother Arrested and Charged with Murder for Throwing 6-Month-Old Baby Girl Out Sixth-Story Window

October 16, 2015 Black

Tenisha Fearon
FACEBOOK

A 27-year-old mother is charged with second-degree murder for allegedly throwing her 6-month-old baby girl out a sixth-story window in New York City.

This is the third baby to have been thrown from a New York City window in three months. In August, the mother of a 3-week-old boy who died after falling from a fourth-floor window in Queens was charged in his death, and in September, Jennifer Berry was charged with second-degree murder after she allegedly threw her newborn baby out a seventh-story window in the Bronx. Both women have pleaded not guilty.

On Thursday, responding officers rushed the infant, identified as Janillah Lawrence, to a nearby hospital, where she was pronounced dead at 2:28 p.m., according to ABC7.

A witness told NBC New York her mother, Tenisha Fearon, was naked and screaming when officers arrived. She refused to answer the door and authorities ended up having to kick it down.

“After she threw them out, she was laying on the floor naked,” the witness said. “After that she started yelling, ‘God help!’ ”
