12 Black Leaders in South Carolina, Including Rep. James Clyburn, to be Honored at S.C. African-American Heritage Foundation

January 13, 2016 Black

More than a dozen African-American leaders and influencers in South Carolina will be honored in an awards ceremony during the annual conference of the South Carolina African-American Heritage Foundation on Friday, Jan. 22.

The conference, “Preserving Our Places in History Through Trial & Triumph,” will begin at 10 a.m. at the South Carolina Archives and History Center at 8301 Parklane Road in Columbia.

Congressman James E. Clyburn, the U.S. representative for South Carolina’s 6th Congressional District for 23 years, and his wife, Emily E. Clyburn, are among the honorees.

Others to be honored include:

  • Columbia Mayor Stephen Benjamin, an attorney who is currently serving as the first African-American mayor of South Carolina’s capital city.
  • Author Dori Sanders, a farmer in Filbert (near York) who expresses life in the South through her publications, including the best-selling novel “Clover” and a cookbook on Southern cooking.
  • Charles Frank Bolden Jr., a retired U.S. Marine Corps major general who was appointed by President Barack Obama as the administrator of NASA in 2009 and who piloted or served as crew commander ofstate recognized the contributions and influence of African Americans during its commemoration of the Civil War.

Registration is underway for the meeting. The fee is $30 for non-SCAAHF members and $25 for members, which covers the entire conference and lunch. Registration forms can be found by visiting http://www.SCAAHeritageFound.org.

For more information on the conference, contact SCAAHF Executive Director Jannie Harriott at 843-332-3589 or scaaheritagefound@gmail.com.

SOURCE: The Times and Democrat
