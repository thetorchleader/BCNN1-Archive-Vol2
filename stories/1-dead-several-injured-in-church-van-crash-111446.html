1 Dead, Several Injured In Church Van Crash

December 16, 2015 National

One person is dead and several are injured in a crash involving a church van Monday night.

It happened just after 8:00 p.m. on Highway 109, one mile north of Colon in Saunders County. That’s in between Wahoo and Cedar Bluffs.

Sheriff Kevin Stukenholtz said the van, driven by 22 year old Theresa Nguyen from Louisiana, was pulling a trailer heading north on Hwy. 109 and pulled into a driveway to do a u-turn, when it was hit broadside by southbound a car.

The male driver of the car, 88 year old Walter Anderson of Wahoo, died and the female passenger, 63 year old Peggy Shapnek of Wahoo, was transported to Bryan Medical Center West in Lincoln and is in critical condition.

Ten occupants in the van were transported to area hospitals by six ambulances. One of the passengers was seriously injured and was transported to UNMC in Omaha. Two other passengers were driven in personal vehicles to a local hosptial. Sheriff Stukenholtz describes all of the passengers in the van as young people. The van was carrying 12 passengers at the time of the crash.

“They were planning on spending the evening in Colon, which is just South of us,” Sheriff Stukenholtz said. “I know the vehicle’s license [plate] is from Minnesota, but I’m told that the passengers were from all over the country.”
