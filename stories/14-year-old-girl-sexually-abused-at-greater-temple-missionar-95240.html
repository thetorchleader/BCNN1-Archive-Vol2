14-Year-Old Girl Sexually Abused at Greater Temple Missionary Church in Birmingham

August 24, 2015 Black

Birmingham Police say a 14-year-old girl was the victim of a sexual abuse at Greater Temple Missionary Church Sunday morning. 

Investigators say a black male suspect entered the church at the 300 block of 4th Avenue West at 11:45 a.m. and entered the women’s restroom.

Lt. Scott Thurmond says the suspect encountered the victim and the sexual abuse occurred.

A person of interest was questioned by detectives before being released to another law enforcement agency on an unrelated outstanding felony warrant.

The investigation is ongoing.

Source: My Fox AL
