40,000 to 50,000 People Expected to Attend National Baptist Convention In Memphis

August 13, 2015 Christian

The nation’s largest African-American religious convention says it could bring 40,000 to 50,000 members to Memphis during Labor Day week.

A convention of that size would eclipse the Church of God in Christ gathering that left Memphis for St. Louis in 2010.

It would be the city’s largest convention of 2015, easily topping the Mid-South Farm & Gin Show, which drew more than 15,000 in February.

A National Baptist Convention USA official said the church expects record attendance in Memphis Sept. 7-11 because the city is an easy drive for many members and because a Mississippian, Jerry Young, is the new president.

The church estimates the convention will have a $45 million to $50 million economic impact on the city, said Marvin Mercer, president of the Nashville-based convention’s 350-church Tennessee unit. The church has 6.5 million to 7.5 million members nationwide and about 100 churches in the Memphis region.

Memphis Mayor A C Wharton, Shelby County Mayor Mark Luttrell and the National Baptist Convention Host Committee have scheduled a news conference 11 a.m. Thursday to announce the convention.

Memphis Convention & Visitors Bureau officials had previously projected the convention at 15,000 to 20,000 people, based on hotel bookings and the 135-year-old event’s historic attendance.

The group’s 2014 annual session in New Orleans, where Young was elected, drew 18,000 to the Crescent City, according to the New Orleans Convention & Visitors Bureau.

Mercer said Memphis’ central location figures into the higher estimate. “The reason they’re using the 50,000 is because of the fact that we’re dealing with people who are members of our convention from bordering states in the South. They can drive in from those states,” Mercer said.

“Also, because of the fact that we have a new president who was elected last year in New Orleans, who is from Jackson, Mississippi. That Mississippi delegation is going to be larger this year than it’s ever been,” Mercer said.

Young is pastor of New Hope Baptist Church.
