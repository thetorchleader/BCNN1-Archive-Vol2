49ers Receive Permission to Speak With Tom Coughlin

January 13, 2016 Sports

Tom Coughlin could have an interview with the 49ers in the near future. (PHOTO CREDIT: Al Bello/Getty Images)

Tom Coughlin definitely isn’t retiring. In fact, he reportedly had a job interview with one team on Monday and might be sitting down for a meeting with another team in the very near future.

The San Francisco 49ers have asked for and received permission to speak with the former Giants coach, according to NFL Media’s Ian Rapoport.

ESPN’s Adam Schefter reports that the Eagles have confirmed they completed their interview with Coughlin Monday afternoon.
