13-Year-Old Entrepreneur Moziah Bridges Lands on TIME’s Most Influential Teens List

October 28, 2015 Business, Lifestyle

Moziah Bridges’ star is on the rise again.

The Memphis born entrepreneur launched his bow tie company Mo’s Bows at the young age of 9-years-old after being unable to find a cool bow tie.

Now at the age of 13, the Shark Tank alum is running a successful business that grosses nearly $200,000 a year in business. He also has partnerships with Neiman Marcus and Cole Haan. WOW!

Bridges just landed on TIME‘s “30 Most Influential Teens of 2015” list. Who else is on the list? He is joining the likes of Malia Obama and Nobel Peace Prize winner Malala Yousafzai. Double WOW!

Keep doing great things Mr. Bridges!
