2015 National Urban League Convention Kicks Off Today In Ft. Lauderdale, Florida

July 29, 2015 Black

Mark your calendars! For four power packed days, political, business, and community leaders will convene you in Fort Lauderdale, Florida for the 2015 National Urban League Conference, July 29 – August 1, 2015.

We look forward to seeing you in South Florida for an inspiring and empowering conference that will provide you with unmatched professional, civic engagement, business development, and networking opportunities.
