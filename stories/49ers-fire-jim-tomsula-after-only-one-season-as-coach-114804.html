49ers Fire Jim Tomsula After Only One Season as Coach

January 3, 2016 Sports

San Francisco 49ers head coach Jim Tomsula during the third quarter against the Detroit Lions at Ford Field.
(Photo: Tim Fuller, USA TODAY Sports)

Jim Tomsula has been fired as head coach of the San Francisco 49ers, the team announced Sunday.

The move means the 49ers will be searching for a new head coach for the second consecutive year after parting with Jim Harbaugh after the 2014 season.

Tomsula, who served as Harbaugh’s defensive line coach, was the hire owner Jed York and general manager Trent Baalke wanted last January, but his one-year stint as head coach was a failure. The 49ers finished 5-11 and were never a factor in the NFC West.

Perhaps Tomsula was never in a position to succeed in his first year as head coach. The challenge only increased when the Niners experienced an exodus of talent last offseason, including the early retirements of linebackers Patrick Willis and Chris Borland, and the decision by the team to cut star pass rusher Aldon Smith in August because of continued off-field issues.

The next coach inherits a quarterback quandary, with starter Colin Kaepernick, who received a six-year contract extension in 2014, embroiled in controversy after a dismal 2015. He was benched at midseason and eventually landed on injured reserve because of a shoulder injury that required surgery. The new coach will need to come to York and Baalke with a solid plan for Kaepernick – or be on board with the idea of getting rid of him and starting over with someone new.

But there are some promising pieces on the roster, including a very good left tackle in Joe Staley, a talented young runner in Carlos Hyde, and good defensive leaders in safety Antoine Bethea and linebacker NaVorro Bowman.
