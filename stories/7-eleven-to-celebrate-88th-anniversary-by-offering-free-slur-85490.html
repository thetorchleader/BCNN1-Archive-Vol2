7-Eleven to Celebrate 88th Anniversary by Offering Free Slurpees on July 11

July 10, 2015 God & Food

7-Eleven is marking the occasion of its eponymous date by offering up free 12-ouce Slurpees to customers on Saturday, July 11 (7/11).

The free giveaway, between the hours of 11 a.m. and 7 p.m., follows the store’s tradition of bestowing some of its most popular items upon patrons to mark its big day. Last year, 7-Eleven gave away free Big Gulps and twinkies.

One can only wonder at the possibilities for next year.
