2016 Stellar Awards to Be Held February 20 In Las Vegas

September 10, 2015 Entertainment

Central City Productions has announced that it will hold the 31st Annual Stellar Awards in Las Vegas once again. The awards were held in “Sin City” for the first time earlier this year. February 20 marks the date. Venue and event information still to be announced.
