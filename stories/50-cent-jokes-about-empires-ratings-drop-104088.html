50 Cent Jokes About ‘Empire’s’ Ratings Drop

October 5, 2015 Black

NEW YORK, NY – SEPTEMBER 10: Curtis ’50 Cent’ Jackson attends Tuesday’s Children Roots of Resilience Gala 2015 on September 10, 2015 in New York City.
PHOTO BY PAUL MORIGI/GETTY IMAGES FOR TUESDAY’S CHILDREN

50 Cent isn’t a fan of Lee Daniels’ Empire. Especially since he already thinks it’s similar to Power, the Starz series he executive produces. Empire’s second season premiered two weeks ago, and although it had excellent ratings, they weren’t near the series’ premiere ratings. 

According to The Wrap, Empire had 16,000,000 million viewers during its premiere, but last Wednesday, the ratings dropped to 13.7 million viewers. Which gave 50 the perfect opportunity to gloat.

“EMPIRES TV RATINGS TOOK A HUGE HIT. Ok I’ll stop now. COOKIE CALL ME BABY. I’ll tell every body to watch the show for you. LOL,” 50 posted on Instagram, which was eventually deleted. The photo 50 shared and made a comment on, came from another Instagram account belonging to Industryonblast_, and it noted that Empire’s ratings drop could be due to all of the “gay stuff”.

“There are 3 million less viewers who tuned into last night’s Empire episode!!!! did you watch it?” the post said. “We could not take the extra gay stuff or celebrity stuff last night!!!!”
