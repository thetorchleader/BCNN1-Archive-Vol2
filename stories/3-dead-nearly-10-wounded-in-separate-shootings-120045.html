3 Dead, Nearly 10 Wounded in Separate Shootings

January 30, 2016 Black

Police officers investigate the scene of a shooting early on Jan. 30, 2016, at the intersection of Kilbourn Avenue and Washington Boulevard where a man was killed. (Alexandra Chachkevitch / Chicago Tribune)

Three people were killed and at least eight people have been wounded in separate shootings since Friday afternoon on the city’s South and West sides, officials said. 

Most recently, a 31-year-old man was found shot to death around 2:55 a.m. Saturday inside a vehicle in the West Garfield Park neighborhood on the West Side, said Officer Ron Gaines, a Chicago police spokesman.

The man was shot in the side during a possibly drive-by shooting at the intersection of Kilbourn Avenue and Washington Boulevard, Gaines said.

The man was pronounced dead at the scene, and no one was in custody, Gaines said.

Police officers blocked off Washington immediately east and west of Kilbourn with yellow and red crime scene tape.

Inside the crime scene west of Kilbourn, a silver Audi SUV stood with its doors opened. The car’s front touched a parked car on the north side of the street.

Police officers were shining flashlights on a cluster of at least four shell casings lying in the middle of Washington immediately east of Kilbourn.

Drivers on the one-way Kilbourn slowed down as they passed the crime scene. Officers eventually fully blocked off the intersection with crime scene tape.

About a dozen people in total gathered nearby.

On the north side of the scene, a man in a black jacket bent down and let out a moan. He sat on the ground next to another man, grieving.

A few moments later, a woman in striped pants and a black jacket walked up to the two men, crying.

“What the f—? Why?” she said, holding up both her arms in the air.

The man in the black jacket walked up to the crime scene tape and tried to pass it to see his friend’s body.

“I need to see my homie. I need to see my homie. I need to see my homie,” the man repeated, his eyes fixed on the silver SUV inside the crime scene.

A pair of police officers stopped him and were joined by a few other officers, who explained that the man couldn’t go past the tape while police are investigating.

“Do you wanna get arrested tonight?” one officer asked.

“Man, I don’t care if I get arrested,” he yelled as the woman and several other friends of the man who was killed walked up to the crime scene.
