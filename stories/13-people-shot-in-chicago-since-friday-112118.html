13 People Shot in Chicago Since Friday

December 19, 2015 Black

Chicago Street Sceens

Thirteen people have been wounded — three of them critically — in shootings across Chicago since Friday night.

The latest attack happened in the Austin neighborhood on the West Side early Saturday.

A 35-year-old man was sitting in a car parked in the 5400 block of West Gladys about 4:50 a.m. when someone in a passing minivan opened fire, according to Chicago Police. He was shot in the chest and arm and taken in critical condition to Mount Sinai Hospital.

A little over an hour earlier, a 17-year-old boy was wounded in the Little Village neighborhood on the Southwest Side. He was outside about 3:40 a.m. in the 2800 block of South St. Louis when he heard shots and realized he’d been hit in the leg, police said. The boy was taken in good condition to Mount Sinai.

About 2:35 a.m., two men were shot at a party in the Gage Park neighborhood. The 19- and 21-year-old were at a party in the 3500 block of West 58th Place when someone opened fire. Both were taken to Stroger Hospital, where the younger man was shot several times and listed in serious condition, and the older was stabilized with wounds to both legs, police said.

An hour earlier, a 24-year-old man was shot as he was in a vehicle in the Back of the Yards. on the South Side. It happened about 1:35 a.m. in the 5400 block of South Racine when another vehicle pulled up and the masked occupants opened fire, striking him in the back, police said. He was taken to Stroger Hospital, where his condition had stabilized.
