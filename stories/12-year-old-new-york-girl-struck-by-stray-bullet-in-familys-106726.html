12-Year-Old New York Girl Struck by Stray Bullet in Family’s Living Room Dies

October 18, 2015 Black

Dejah Joyner, 12, who lived in this house

The 12-year-old Long Island girl who was struck in her family’s living room by a bullet fired from outside the house has died, the authorities said on Sunday.

The girl, identified as Dejah Joyner, died on Saturday evening at the hospital where she had been taken in critical condition on Friday with a single gunshot wound to the head.

The police were called to the home on Dartmouth Street in Hempstead, N.Y., around 5 p.m. on Friday, after the girl was shot. Later, detectives determined that a gunshot had been fired from the street, the police said.

There have been no arrests in the case, the police said, and officials have not released any information about a possible assailant. Investigators have asked for the public’s assistance in the case.

On Friday, some residents said the shooting in their quiet neighborhood reminded them of the killing of a 17-year-old boy in 2013.

“Right now it is very, very scary,” James Greene, who has lived in the neighborhood since 1964, said on Friday. “One fellow was killed two years ago, and now this happened next door.”

Source: The New York Times | RICK ROJAS
