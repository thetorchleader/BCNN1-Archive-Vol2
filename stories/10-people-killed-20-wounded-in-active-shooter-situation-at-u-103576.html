15 People Killed, 20 Wounded in Shooting at Umpqua Community College in Oregon; Shooter is Down

October 1, 2015 National

 

An active shooter is reportedly on campus at Umpqua Community College in Roseburg.

The Oregon State Police confirmed at least 10 people were dead and 20 were wounded.

The campus is on lock down.

KOIN 6 will have more information when it becomes available.
