1 Person Dead, 2 Officers Injured in Shooting in Savannah, GA

October 28, 2015 National

The Georgia Bureau of Investigation has confirmed that one person is dead and two Metro officers were injured in a shooting overnight in Savannah.

The Savannah-Chatham Metropolitan Police Department says the shooting happened around midnight at the 37th Street Connector and Ogeechee Road.

Police say the two officers were transported to Memorial with non-life-threatening injuries. The GBI later confirmed that the other person that was shot died.

Both Mayor Edna Jackson and District 1 Alderman Van Johnson came out to the scene after being contacted by the city manager. WTOC asked the city leaders what happened and they said little is known about this situation and it is now in the hands of the GBI.

City leaders also spoke of their concerns for the Metro officers involved, and how thankful they are of their efforts in keeping the Cuyler-Brownsville neighborhood safe.

“I am probably not at liberty to say, but when we talk about law enforcement it is times like these that we have to think about the personal sacrifices that people who put on a uniform make,” said Van Johnson, District 1 Alderman.

Mayor Jackson did go to the hospital to visit with the officers. She spoke about her concerns for the city.

“I just don’t know if there is a perfect answer of what to do here in our city. All I can say is that our men and women in blue are doing everything that they can,” the Mayor said.

The GBI is leading the investigation.
