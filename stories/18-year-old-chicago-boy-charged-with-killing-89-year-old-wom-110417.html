18-Year-Old Chicago Boy Charged with Killing 89-Year-Old Woman and Setting her House on Fire

November 6, 2015 Black

Trae Chatmon, 18, of the 4900 block of South St. Lawrence Avenue, is charged with first-degree murder. (Cook County sheriff’s photo)

An 18-year-old man has been charged with murder in the death of an 89-year-old woman, accused of breaking into her South Side home, hitting and stabbing her, then ransacking her apartment and setting it on fire, authorities said.

Trae Chatmon, 18, of the 4900 block of South St. Lawrence Avenue, is charged with first-degree murder. He was already in Cook County Jail in another case when the new charge was filed, police said. He appeared Thursday afternoon at a bail hearing where Cook County Judge Laura Sullivan ordered him held on $1 million bail in the killing of Josephine Johnson.

Police said Chatmon forced his way into Johnson’s home in the 5100 block of South Michigan Avenue around 2:55 a.m. Oct. 26. He hit Johnson in the head and stabbed her several times, according to police. Then he set the apartment on fire and fled with several stolen items, police said.

Neighbors awoke to the smell of smoke and attempted to check on Johnson. One neighbor used a spare key to enter the home and saw fire coming out of the kitchen area and Johnson lying on the floor, prosecutors said.

“She didn’t have any enemies,” said Charles Morris, who lives in the basement apartment. “I knew Miss Josephine for maybe 15 years. She was a nice lady. She had no children.”

When police and firefighters arrived, officials found Johnson lying outside of her bedroom with multiple stab wounds to her body and blunt force injuries to her head. She also had suffered burns to the upper torso, prosecutors said Thursday.
