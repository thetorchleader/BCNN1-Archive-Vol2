10 Ways Coconut Oil Can Change Your Life

February 28, 2016 From Around the Block

A lot of people don’t know yet all benefits about coconut oil. Most of them use this type of oil only for cooking because they already know it is one of the most healthy oils in the market.

If you are one of them, you must know that you can also swap out some of your beauty products for this wonder oil. The positive rewards it brings to your life upon use are just too amazing to go unnoticed. Just Keep reading!

Let’s start with the first product.

You can make your own exfoliator by mixing together 1/4 cup granulated sugar and 1/2 cup coconut oil. Put it in a jar or container and use it, you’ll notice that your skin will look, feel and smell better than ever before, and of course you’ll save money over store-bought products.
