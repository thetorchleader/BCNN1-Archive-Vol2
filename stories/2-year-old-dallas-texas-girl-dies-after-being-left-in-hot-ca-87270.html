2-Year-Old Dallas, Texas, Girl Dies After Being Left in Hot Car

July 19, 2015 Black

Police are investigating the death of a 2-year-old child who was allegedly left behind in a hot car after her family returned from an outing. 

According to Child Abuse Unit investigators, the child’s parents said they returned from a family outing to Fair Park with their children Friday afternoon and went inside their home to take a nap.

“(The girl’s) parents stated that they believed all children had exited the vehicle when they returned home,” police spokeswoman Senior Cpl. Melinda Gutierrez said in a written statement.

The child’s father said that he later went outside to work on his car and found her strapped in a child safety seat.

“He immediately removed the child from the vehicle and took her inside, where he called 911 as the mother performed CPR for approximately 30 minutes,” Gutierrez said.

The police spokeswoman said the mother then drove the child to Children’s Medical Center hospital, where the girl was pronounced dead at 7:05 p.m.

The National Weather Service said the high temperature at Love Field in Dallas reached 98 degrees on Friday afternoon. KidsandCars.org says that on average, 38 children die each year from heat-related deaths after being trapped in vehicles.

Child Protective Services has been alerted, but no charges have been filed, police said.

Source: USA Today | Sebastian Robertson, WFAA-TV, Dallas-Fort Worth
