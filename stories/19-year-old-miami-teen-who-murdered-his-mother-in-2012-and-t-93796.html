19-Year-Old Miami Teen Who Murdered his Mother in 2012 and Threw a Party With Her Dead Body in Next Room Gets 28 Years in Prison

August 17, 2015 Black

Kit Darrant
LOCAL 10 SCREENSHOT

In March 2012, Kit Darrant threw a party for over 60 of his high school friends. The teens all from Dr. Michael M. Krop Senior High School all partied in the apartment where Darrant lived with his mother. At one point, a student asked Darrant about a strange smell. He told her it was probably the trash.

What none of the students partying that night knew is that Darrant had reportedly killed his mother with a butcher knife and left her decomposing body in the room for some eight days before it was found.

On Friday, the now 19-year-old Darrant plead guilty to second-degree murder for killing his mother, 35-year-old Renette Emile, and will spend the next 28 years in prison, the Miami Herald reports.

According to the newspaper, Darrant argued with his mother on the night of her death. He reportedly strangled her and then, unsure if she was dead, he grabbed a butcher knife from the kitchen and stabbed her over 100 times.

Darrant reportedly hid the body in her room covered in blankets an laundry detergent to hide the smell. As his mom’s body was decaying in her bedroom the then 16-year-old spent the next eight days, shopping, skipping school, even throwing a party for his friends.

Darrant’s sister and her boyfriend reportedly found they body after they grew suspicious of her mother’s whereabouts.
