24 Breakfast Casseroles for the Holidays

December 25, 2015 God & Food

Never take individual egg orders again.

Looking for more breakfast ideas? Try these pancakes you just won’t be able to resist.

Cheesy Tater Tot Breakfast Bake
Swap out hash browns for tater tots and top with crumbled bacon for all kinds of irresistible cheesiness. Get the recipe at Chew Out Loud.
Courtesy of Chew Out Loud

Bacon, Egg, and Cheese Breakfast Bread
The three morning essentials—BEC—baked into one amazing monkey bread. Get the recipe from Delish.
Ethan Calabrese

Chocolate Chip Pancake Casserole
We’re dying over this pancake casserole: Layers of fluffy pancakes, sprinkled with chocolate chips, and showered in powdered sugar. Get the recipe from Delish.
Ethan Calabrese

Eggs Benedict Skillet Casserole
Always order eggs benny when out to brunch? Recreate the fave at home with this one-pan dish. Get the recipe from Delish.
Ethan Calabrese

Cinnamon Roll Casserole
Store-bought biscuits combined in a baking dish with an egg-milk mixture is officially the easiest way ever to serve cinnamon rolls to a crowd. Get the recipe from Delish.
Ethan Calabrese

Eggnog French Toast Bake
Ring in the holiday season with this nutmeg-spiced eggnog overnight French toast. Get the recipe from Delish.
Ethan Calabrese

Overnight Gingerbread French Toast Breakfast Bake
With this decadent breakfast (that you prep the night before) in the oven, your family won’t know whether to head for the tree or the kitchen on Christmas morning. Get the recipe from Averie Cooks.
Averie Cooks

Tater Tot Enchilada Bake
Anything involving tater tots is sure to be an instant breakfast hit. Add a fried egg on top and you’ll have everyone asking for seconds. Get the recipe from Brunch Time Baker.
Brunch Time Baker

Overnight Eggnog French Toast Casserole
Sneak a little eggnog into your Christmas breakfast with this baked take on French toast. Get the recipe from Five Heart Home.
Five Heart Home

Breakfast Casserole
Get all the flavor of an apple fritter without the individual prep. Get the recipe from Wonky Wonderful.
Wonky Wonderful

Pizza Frittata
You’ll have some new-found frittata fans if you top and slice them like pizzas. Get the recipe from Minimalist Baker.
Minimalist Baker

Giant Cinnamon Roll Cake
Don’t waste time rolling individual cinnamon rolls — instead, make one giant cinnamon roll “cake.” Get the recipe from Sally’s Baking Addiction.
Sally’s Baking Addiction
