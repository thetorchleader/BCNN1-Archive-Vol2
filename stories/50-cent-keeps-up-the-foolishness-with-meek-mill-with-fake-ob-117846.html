50 Cent Keeps Up the Foolishness with Meek Mill with Fake Obituary

January 19, 2016 Black

Meek Mill is dead. Or so says 50 Cent, who intensified his feud with the fellow rapper on social media Monday with a fake obituary for Mill. 

The obituary begins, “In Loving Memory of Robert Rihmeek Williams, aka Meek Mill, May 6, 1987 – January 17, 2016.”

It quickly deviates dramatically from the standard obituary tone, however.

“Meek Mill pass [sic] Sunday after a long battle of taking L’s [losses] from 50 Cent of G-Unit,” the faux-bituary reads. “He is survived by Nicki Minaj, Rick Ross, Dream Chasers & Maybach Music Group. He will not be greatly miss [sic].”

The obituary notes that food for Mill’s funeral service will be provided by Wingstop, with music by 50 Cent, Drake and Future.

The fake obit is the latest salvo in a beef between the two rappers. After Mill released a track “Gave ‘Em Hope,” which seems to make oblique reference to 50 Cent ex-girlfriend Vivica Fox’s suggestion late last year that her former beau might be gay.
