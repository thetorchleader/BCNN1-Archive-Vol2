5 People Found Dead in Home in Broken Arrow, OK; 2 Teenagers Arrested

July 23, 2015 National, zTransfer-UCN

Police on scene of apparent quintuple homicide in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma on July 22, 2015 (KOTV)

Police in eastern Oklahoma say five people have been found dead and two teenagers are in custody.

In a news release, Broken Arrow police Sgt. Thomas Cooper says officers were called to a home around 11:30 p.m. Wednesday for an unknown problem.

The officers found the five bodies and a juvenile who was brought to a hospital in critical condition, reports CBS Tulsa affiliate KOTV. Another juvenile was found unharmed.

The station cites a source as saying the hospitalized victim is a 12- or 13-year-old girl who was stabbed several times.

The release says a 16-year-old and an 18-year-old, who are both related to the victims, were detained.

The release calls the case a homicide investigation. No other details were immediately available.

Broken Arrow is southeast of Tulsa.
