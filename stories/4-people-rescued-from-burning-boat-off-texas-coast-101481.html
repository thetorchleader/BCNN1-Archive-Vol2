4 People Rescued From Burning Boat Off Texas Coast

September 20, 2015 National

This photo provided by the United States Coast Guard shows a boat on fire. Some Good Samaritans helped rescue four passengers off the South Galveston Jetty in Texas on September 20, 2015. (PHOTO CREDIT: United States Coast Guard)

The Coast Guard and some Good Samaritans rescued four people when their boat caught fire Saturday off the South Galveston Jetty in Texas.

The Coast Guard said it received a distress call around 6:30 p.m. about a boat on fire. The boat was carrying a man, woman, a 6-year-old boy, and a fourth person.
