13 Shot, 2 Dead, in Chicago Since Monday

August 18, 2015 National

Two men were shot to death in less than half an hour within eight blocks of each other, and at least 11 other people were wounded in shootings citywide since Monday morning, police said.

A 37-year-old man was killed on the porch of a graystone two-flat in East Garfield Park at about 9:30 p.m. Monday in a shooting that also injured a 34-year-old woman.

Officers investigated the scene in torrential rain, crowding together on the small porch around the body to stay dry.

Police blocked off the building, in the 3800 block of West Monroe Street, along with a patch of long, dead grass in front of it.

Other graystones on the block had green lawns and well-kept gardens, but the area near the crime scene was covered in litter: an empty bottle of Remy Martin cognac, a container of mayonnaise, a plastic to-go box streaked with nacho cheese. A neighbor said the house had been abandoned and now was frequented by drug users.

At about 10 p.m., sirens rang out over the thunder, and a uniformed officer jogged out of the crime scene and on to the sidewalk.

“Uh-oh, another one,” he said to himself.

A 34-year-old woman had been found in the 4000 block of West Lake Street with a gunshot wound to the arm. She told police she was with the man at the graystone when she heard shots and felt pain, then ran from the scene.

She was taken to Mount Sinai hospital, and her condition was stabilized, said Officer Amina Greer, a Chicago Police spokeswoman.

Less than 30 minutes before the shooting, a 33-year-old man was fatally shot on another porch about half a mile away, in the West Garfield Park neighborhood.

Police found the man on a porch in the 4300 block of West Van Buren Street with gunshot wounds to the head and torso. He was taken to Stroger Hospital in critical condition, where he was pronounced dead about an hour after he was shot, said Sgt. Al Stinites of Chicago Police News Affairs.

In the most recent non-fatal shooting, a 30-year-old man was struck in the leg in the Gresham neighborhood, Greer said.

He was in the 7500 block of South Emerald Avenue at 12:45 a.m. Tuesday when someone approached and fired shots. He went to Advocate Christ Medical Center, where his condition was stabilized.

A man and a woman were wounded in a shooting in Grand Crossing at 11:05 p.m., police said.

The woman, 27, was in her car in the 1300 block of East 72nd Street. A 24-year-old man was standing outside the car talking to her when a dark sedan drove up and someone inside fired shots, Greer said.
