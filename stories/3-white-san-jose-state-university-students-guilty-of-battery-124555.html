3 White San Jose State University Students Guilty of Battery on Black Freshman, but Dodge Hate Crime Charge

February 22, 2016 Black, National, U. S.

Donald Williams Jr. and family
Karl Mondon/ Bay Area News Group

Three white students from San Jose State University were found guilty of misdemeanor battery on Monday, but were absolved of a hate crime. The charges stem from a 2013 incident in which the students—Colin Warren, Logan Beaschler, and Joseph “Brett” Bomgardner—allegedly put a bike lock around the neck of black teenager Donald Williams Jr. while attempting to lock him in a closet and display the Confederate flag in a suite they shared. Prosecutors also alleged that the students referred to Williams as “three-fifths” and “fraction.”
