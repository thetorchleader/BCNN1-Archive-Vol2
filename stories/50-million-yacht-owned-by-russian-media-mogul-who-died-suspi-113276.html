$50 Million Yacht Owned by Russian Media Mogul Who Died Suspiciously in Washington Floats Aimlessly in Australian Waters

December 25, 2015 World

A superyacht reportedly owned by Vladimir Putin’s former chief propagandist has been deserted at a Brisbane marina after he suddenly died from a ‘heart attack’ at a modest Washington hotel.

Mikhail Lesin, 57, was found dead in early November and there was speculation he was murdered on Moscow’s orders or even faked his death.

His yacht Serenity, a 55m monster produced by the Dutch Heesen shipyards, was photographed stranded at the Rivergate Marina on the Brisbane River on Christmas Eve.

The Courier Mail first reported the ship belonged to Mr Lesin. Records show it arrived in Brisbane from Cairns on September 14 and has been moored at the refitting yard ever since.

Mr Lesin has been described as a ‘Svengali figure for Putin’, who was alleged to have menaced the Russian media into idolizing the strongman president as his press minister from 1999 to 2004.

He later founded the pro-Russian English language news channel RT, which is now seen by critics as something of a propaganda arm for Putin’s government.

The Daily Mail exclusively revealed in November that only weeks before his death was announced, Mr Lesin fathered a child with glamorous model and flight attendant Victoria Rakhimbayeva.

Pictures of Ms Rakhimbaveya showed her travelling around the world on a boat – although it was unclear whether she was aboard the same exclusive vessel.

The luxury yacht’s interior was designed by architects, according to luxury yacht news source Charter World, and contains eleven different types of marble, including Iranian gold onyx.

The boat has been described as having a ‘celestial atmosphere’ and can comfortably accommodate more than twelve guests in its six cabins spread over four floors.

Inside the stateroom – where the boat’s owner would likely live – the ceiling features painting of ‘a sky adorned with fluffy clouds’.
